# 5 Productivity Workflows That Actually Work

Finding a productivity system that sticks is harder than it looks. Here are five workflows that have stood the test of time.

## 1. Time Blocking

Reserve specific blocks of time for specific tasks. This prevents the constant context switching that destroys focus.

**How to implement:**
- Block your calendar in 90-minute chunks
- Group similar tasks together
- Protect your deep work blocks aggressively

## 2. The Two-Minute Rule

If a task takes less than two minutes, do it immediately. This prevents small tasks from piling up.

**Why it works:**
- Reduces mental overhead
- Maintains momentum
- Keeps your inbox and mind clear

## 3. The Weekly Review

Every week, spend 30 minutes reviewing what you accomplished and planning the week ahead.

**What to cover:**
- Review completed tasks
- Process emails and messages
- Update project status
- Set priorities for next week

## 4. The Pomodoro Technique

Work in focused 25-minute sprints, then take 5-minute breaks. After four sprints, take a longer break.

**Benefits:**
- Builds focus stamina
- Creates natural break points
- Makes large tasks feel manageable

## 5. Capture Everything

Never rely on memory alone. Have a trusted system to capture every idea, task, and commitment.

**Tools to consider:**
- Note-taking apps (Notion, Apple Notes, Obsidian)
- Task managers (Todoist, Things, Asana)
- Voice memos for on-the-go

The key is consistency—pick one workflow and stick with it for at least 30 days before judging its effectiveness.
